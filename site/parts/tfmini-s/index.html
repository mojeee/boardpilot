Benewake TFmini-S LiDAR (UART) with ESP32

Supply 5 V Bus GPIO Measures distance

Pins and ESP32 wiring

PinRoleESP32 pinNotes
5VPowerVINRed wire. Needs 5 V: use VIN. Check the wire colours on your cable
GNDGroundGNDBlack wire
TXOutput (the part drives it)D25Green wire. UART: the part sends data here. Connect to an ESP32 RX pin (115200 baud, 3.3 V level)
RXInput (the ESP32 drives it)D26White wire. UART: the part receives data here. Connect to an ESP32 TX pin

Suggested wiring for the 30-pin ESP32 DevKit, avoiding flash, input-only and strapping pins. BoardPilot checks it for you as you wire.

This module is sold for 5 V. Its outputs may be 5 V: use a level shifter or a voltage divider on ESP32 inputs.
