SIM800L GSM/GPRS module (2G) con ESP32

Alimentazione 3.4-4.4 V Bus GPIO

Pin e collegamento all’ESP32

PinRuoloPin ESP32Note
NETNon collegato all’ESP32—Antenna connection
VCCAlimentazione3V33.4 to 4.4 V with peaks up to 2 A. Use a Li-ion cell or a buck converter, not the ESP32 pins
RSTIngresso (lo pilota l’ESP32)D25Reset, active LOW
RXDIngresso (lo pilota l’ESP32)D26UART: the module receives here (2.8 V logic). Connect to an ESP32 TX pin through a divider
TXDUscita (la pilota il componente)D27UART: the part sends data here. Connect to an ESP32 RX pin. Auto-baud: try 9600 or 115200
GNDMassaGNDConnect to ESP32 GND too
RINGUscita (la pilota il componente)D32LOW on an incoming call or SMS, optional
DTRNon collegato all’ESP32—Sleep control, optional
MIC+Non collegato all’ESP32—Microphone, optional
MIC-Non collegato all’ESP32—
SPK+Non collegato all’ESP32—Speaker, optional
SPK-Non collegato all’ESP32—

Collegamento consigliato per l’ESP32 DevKit a 30 pin, evitando i pin della flash, di sola lettura e di strapping. BoardPilot lo controlla mentre colleghi.

Fonti

  • SIMCom SIM800 series hardware design, Power supply 3.4 to 4.4 V, burst current up to 2 A; UART at 2.8 V levels
