SSD1309 2.42" OLED 128x64 (SPI) con ESP32

Alimentazione 3.3-5 V Bus SPI

Pin e collegamento all’ESP32

PinRuoloPin ESP32Note
GNDMassaGNDPin order varies between boards: match by the printed label.
VCCAlimentazione3V33.3 V or 5 V (on-board regulator on most boards)
SCKClock SPID18SPI clock. Some boards print SCL: still SPI, not I2C
SDASPI MOSID23SPI data (MOSI), not I2C
RESIngresso (lo pilota l’ESP32)D25Reset. Needed: the SSD1309 will not start without a reset pulse
DCIngresso (lo pilota l’ESP32)D26Data / command select
CSChip select SPID5

Collegamento consigliato per l’ESP32 DevKit a 30 pin, evitando i pin della flash, di sola lettura e di strapping. BoardPilot lo controlla mentre colleghi.

Fonti

  • Solomon Systech SSD1309 datasheet, 4-wire serial interface: CS#, D/C#, SCLK (D0), SDIN (D1); RES# low pulse at power-up
