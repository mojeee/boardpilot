Pins to avoid or use with care

Strapping pins: 2, 9, 8

Their level at reset changes how the board boots. Usually fine after boot.

USB serial: TX (GPIO21), RX (GPIO20)

Used for uploading and the serial monitor. Anything connected here can break uploads.

Good pins for LEDs, buttons and signals

4, 5, 6, 7, 10, 3, 0, 1

Default pins in Arduino

I2C
SDA = 8, SCL = 9. Any output-capable pins work with Wire.begin(sda, scl). Any GPIO can be I2C on the ESP32-C3. Arduino's defaults, GPIO 8 (SDA) and GPIO 9 (SCL), are strapping pins and GPIO 8 also drives the RGB LED.
SPI
MOSI = 6, MISO = 5, SCK = 4, CS = 7.
Analog inputs
0, 1, 3, 4, 2 (0–3.3 V)

How to flash it

If uploading does not start, hold BOOT, press and release RST, then release BOOT.

Questions

Which pins are I2C on the ESP32-C3-DevKitM-1?

SDA is 8 and SCL is 9 (the defaults of the Arduino Wire library). You can move I2C to other pins with Wire.begin(sda, scl).

Which ESP32-C3-DevKitM-1 pins should I avoid?

TX, RX (USB serial link).

Is the ESP32-C3-DevKitM-1 5 V tolerant?

No. The pins take at most 3.3 V. Use a level shifter or a voltage divider for 5 V signals.

Which pins can read analog voltage on the ESP32-C3-DevKitM-1?

0, 1, 3, 4, 2, from 0 to 3.3 V.

How do I upload code to the ESP32-C3-DevKitM-1?

In the Arduino IDE pick the board esp32:esp32:esp32c3 (core esp32:esp32). If uploading does not start, hold BOOT, press and release RST, then release BOOT.
