WeAct Black Pill STM32F411CE pinout

A tiny, low-cost STM32F411 board with USB-C. Fast (100 MHz), breadboard friendly, 3.3 V logic, and most pins tolerate 5 V.

At a glance

Chip
STM32F411CE (STM32F411CEU6)
Processor
Arm Cortex-M4 with FPU at 100 MHz, 128 KB SRAM, 512 KB flash
Logic level
3.3 V. Not 5 V tolerant unless marked below.
Flashing tool
STM32CubeProgrammer, stlink or dfu-util
Arduino board (FQBN)

Pins to avoid or use with care

Strapping pins: B2

Their level at reset changes how the board boots. Usually fine after boot.

Native USB: A11, A12

The USB data lines. Using them breaks USB uploads and the serial monitor.

Debug port: SCK, DIO

SWD/JTAG. Using them can stop a debugger from reaching the chip.

Used on the board: A0, C15, C14

Already connected to something on the board (see the notes in the table).

Good pins for LEDs, buttons and signals

B12, B13, B14, B15, A8, B10, B0, B1, B8, B9, A15

Default pins in Arduino

I2C
SDA = B7, SCL = B6. Only these pins work with the Wire library defaults. Wire uses PB7 for SDA and PB6 for SCL. Only specific STM32 pin pairs can do I2C (for example PB9/PB8 with Wire.setSDA/setSCL).
SPI
MOSI = A7, MISO = A6, SCK = A5, CS = A4.
Analog inputs
A1, A2, A3, B0, B1, A4, A5, A6, A7, A0 (0–3.3 V)
5 V tolerant pins
B10, B2, B1, B0, A7, A6, A5, A4, A3, A2, A1, C15, C14, C13, B12, B13, B14, B15, A8, A9, A10, A11, A12, A15, B3, B4, B6, B7, B8, B9, SCK, DIO

How to flash it

To upload over USB, hold BOOT0, press and release NRST, then release BOOT0 (DFU mode). An ST-LINK on the SWD pins also works.

Questions

Which pins are I2C on the WeAct Black Pill STM32F411CE?

SDA is B7 and SCL is B6 (the defaults of the Arduino Wire library). Only certain pins support I2C in hardware, so use these unless you know your core allows others.

Which WeAct Black Pill STM32F411CE pins should I avoid?

A11, A12 (native USB); SCK, DIO (debug port); A0, C15, C14 (used on the board).

Is the WeAct Black Pill STM32F411CE 5 V tolerant?

The chip runs at 3.3 V, but these pins tolerate 5 V inputs: B10, B2, B1, B0, A7, A6, A5, A4, A3, A2, A1, C15, C14, C13, B12, B13, B14, B15, A8, A9, A10, A11, A12, A15, B3, B4, B6, B7, B8, B9, SCK, DIO. The others take at most 3.3 V.

Which pins can read analog voltage on the WeAct Black Pill STM32F411CE?

A1, A2, A3, B0, B1, A4, A5, A6, A7, A0, from 0 to 3.3 V.

How do I upload code to the WeAct Black Pill STM32F411CE?

In the Arduino IDE pick the board STMicroelectronics:stm32:GenF4:pnum=BLACKPILL_F411CE,usb=CDCgen,upload_method=dfuMethod (core STMicroelectronics:stm32). To upload over USB, hold BOOT0, press and release NRST, then release BOOT0 (DFU mode). An ST-LINK on the SWD pins also works.
